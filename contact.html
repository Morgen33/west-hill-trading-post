Customer care

Write to Russ.

russ@westhilltradingpost.com

There is no storefront and no cart. West Hill works by letter. He answers.

Shipping

Orders leave the bench within two working days. Domestic ground shipping is free over $150. International is quoted when you write, and it goes tracked. Duties sit with the receiver. Anything made to order has its own lead time, and Russ will say so before you commit.

Returns

Unused goods can come back within 30 days for a full refund, in their original packing. Made-to-order and personalised pieces are final sale. Write first and you will get return instructions the same day.

Repairs

Russ will put right anything that carries the mark. Ordinary wear is covered for the first two years. After that, the charge is materials only. Send the piece with a note about what went wrong.
