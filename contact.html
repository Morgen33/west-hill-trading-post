Shipping & delivery

Orders leave the bench within two working days. Domestic ground shipping is free over $150; international is quoted at checkout and sent tracked. Duties are the responsibility of the receiver. Anything made to order carries its own lead time, stated on the product page before you buy.

Returns

Unused goods can come back within 30 days for a full refund, in their original packing. Made-to-order and personalised pieces are final sale. Write to us first and we will send return instructions the same day.

Repairs & care

Russ will put right anything that carries the mark. Ordinary wear is covered for the first two years; after that we charge for materials only. Send the piece with a note describing what went wrong.
