The maker

The trade that built his life.

Twenty years a welder. Now teaching it. The cuffs come off the same pair of hands.

Russ Snyder

Russ Snyder spent 20 years as a NYC instructional seal welder before stepping into the high school classroom to teach the trade that built his life. Raised in New Jersey, Russ always knew he wanted to teach. Not because welding was his only option, but because he wanted young people to understand that it could be theirs.

He is passionate about passing down the hard-earned skills, discipline, and lessons the job taught him, especially to students who have been written off, underestimated, or made to feel like they aren't smart enough to succeed.

It doesn't matter what anyone has called you or made you believe about yourself. You can learn, you can build, and you can accomplish anything you decide to pursue.

West Hill Trading Post is the shop. The mark on it says Est. 2024, Texas. There is no storefront and no class for sale on this site. You write, and Russ answers.

Materials

Copper, stainless, and bronze, worked by hand and finished unlacquered so the metal keeps moving after it leaves the bench. Copper will darken. That is not damage. A soft cloth is the whole care routine, unless something actually breaks.

The Sundown carries all three metals. The Levi is copper and stainless. The Ranch Hand is copper alone, and it is not stamped. Horseshoes, an Indian head, and TEXAS are struck when a piece calls for them.

Repairs

He fixes what he makes.

Send a piece back and Russ will put it right. Ordinary wear is covered for the first two years. After that, the charge is materials and nothing else.
