The trade that built his life

Russ Snyder. Twenty years a welder, now teaching the trade to the students everyone else counted out.

Russ Snyder

Russ Snyder spent 20 years as a NYC instructional seal welder before stepping into the high school classroom to teach the trade that built his life. Raised in New Jersey, Russ always knew he wanted to teach. Not because welding was his only option, but because he wanted young people to understand that it could be theirs.

He is passionate about passing down the hard-earned skills, discipline, and lessons the job taught him, especially to students who have been written off, underestimated, or made to feel like they aren't smart enough to succeed.

It doesn't matter what anyone has called you or made you believe about yourself. You can learn, you can build, and you can accomplish anything you decide to pursue.

Materials

Copper, brass and nickel silver, worked by hand and finished unlacquered so the metal keeps moving after it leaves the bench. Every piece carries the struck mark.
