The Open Bench

Room for more hands. Introductions are open.

For artisans

Room for more hands

West Hill opened with one bench and two pieces. It was never meant to stay that way. We are quietly looking for a small number of artisans to set up alongside Russ, each keeping their own name, their own mark and their own standard.

  1. 01

    Made by your hands

    Work you make yourself, in small numbers. No resale, no drop-ship, nothing bought in and re-labelled.

  2. 02

    Your name stays yours

    You are listed as the maker, not absorbed into a house brand. The mark says who made it and the card says where it came from.

  3. 03

    A short bench, on purpose

    A handful of makers rather than a marketplace. It stays small so every piece on the site can be stood behind.

Tell us what you make and how you make it. A few photographs help.

Write to us

What happens next

Russ reads every introduction himself, so it takes a little while. If the work fits, the next step is a conversation about how you make it, what a run looks like, and what you want to charge. Nothing is exclusive and nothing is locked in.

What it costs you

Terms are settled one maker at a time and written down plainly before anything is listed. Ask and you will get a straight answer.
