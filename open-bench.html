For artisans

The Open Bench.

Room for more hands. Introductions are open.

  1. 01

    Made by your hands

    Work you make yourself, in small numbers. No resale, no drop-ship, nothing bought in and re-labelled.

  2. 02

    Your name stays yours

    You are listed as the maker, not absorbed into a house brand. The mark says who made it.

  3. 03

    A short bench, on purpose

    A handful of makers rather than a marketplace. It stays small so every piece on the site can be stood behind.

Tell us what you make and how you make it. A few photographs help. Russ reads every introduction himself, so it takes a little while. If the work fits, the next step is a conversation. Nothing is exclusive and nothing is locked in. Terms are settled one maker at a time and written down before anything is listed.
