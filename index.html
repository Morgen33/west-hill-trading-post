What we are building

A trading post, not a marketplace

West Hill Trading Post is a small shop for work made by hand. It starts with Russ Snyder's bracelets and bandanas, and it grows one maker at a time.

Every piece is made by the person whose name is on it, in numbers small enough that somebody can stand behind each one. We would rather stay small and mean it than get big and stop.

Materials The Maker Marks Origins

Welder — Teacher

Russ Snyder

Russ Snyder spent 20 years as a NYC instructional seal welder before stepping into the high school classroom to teach the trade that built his life. Raised in New Jersey, Russ always knew he wanted to teach. Not because welding was his only option, but because he wanted young people to understand that it could be theirs.
