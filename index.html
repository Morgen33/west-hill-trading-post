02 — The finish

Left bare, on purpose.

Polished by hand and left unlacquered. Copper darkens where a wrist and the weather reach it. That is the metal doing what copper does.

West Hill is a trading post, not a marketplace. One maker, small numbers, and a name on the work.

How the metals behave

Copper

The red metal. It darkens with wear, and a soft cloth brings the light back when you want it.

Stainless

The bright metal. It holds the edge of the weave and stays where you polished it.

Bronze

The warm metal in the Sundown, sitting between the copper and the steel.

03 — Outside

Made to leave the shop.

Copper and stainless, set down on a cactus for the photograph. The women's cuff in those two metals is the Levi.

The Levi, $200

Welder, teacher

Russ Snyder

Russ Snyder spent 20 years as a NYC instructional seal welder before stepping into the high school classroom to teach the trade that built his life. Raised in New Jersey, he always knew he wanted to teach. Not because welding was his only option, but because he wanted young people to understand that it could be theirs.

The trading post is his. The mark says Est. 2024, Texas. He still teaches.
